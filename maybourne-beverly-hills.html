Beverly Hills Favorite

The Maybourne Beverly Hills is the polished, walkable Beverly Hills stay for shopping, dining, and easy luxury.

The Maybourne sits in the heart of Beverly Hills, close to Rodeo Drive and Beverly Canon Gardens. It feels refined, international, and very easy to use for clients who want Beverly Hills access without needing the full Hollywood nostalgia of the Pink Palace.

What makes it unique

It is one of the easiest Beverly Hills hotels for clients who want to walk, shop, dine, and still have a true luxury base.

The Maybourne works when the trip is built around Beverly Hills lifestyle: Rodeo Drive, beauty, dining, appointments, private shopping, and a rooftop pool day between everything. It feels polished without being stiff.

Best For

Walkable Beverly Hills

Perfect for shopping, dining, and clients who want to stay in the middle of Beverly Hills.

Known For

Rooftop pool and polished suites

The rooftop and larger suites make the hotel feel more resort-like than the location suggests.

Dining

The Terrace and Maybourne Bar

Strong for relaxed meals, cocktails, and a Beverly Hills social rhythm.

Client Type

Shopping and lifestyle clients

Great for polished couples, families, and clients who want the city at their fingertips.

Rooms and suites

The suite product is the reason I would lean into this hotel for longer Beverly Hills stays. The larger suites give clients more room to settle in, and the rooftop pool adds that easy downtime piece between appointments, meals, and shopping.

Dining and activities

The Terrace is the easy all-day Beverly Hills restaurant, The Maybourne Bar handles cocktails, and the hotel puts clients close to Rodeo Drive, private shopping, wellness appointments, beauty, and some of LA’s best restaurants.

My honest take

The Maybourne is not trying to be old Hollywood. It is cleaner, more international, and extremely useful. I would use it when the client wants convenience, polish, and a Beverly Hills stay that feels current.
