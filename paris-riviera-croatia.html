Paris, Riviera, and Croatia

Paris to Cannes, Monaco, and a final Adriatic close in Croatia.

This proposal starts in Paris with immediate polish, intensifies along the South of France with glamour and speed, then softens in Croatia with sea-facing ease, yacht options, and a more restorative last act.

Proposal Overview

A layered European summer with a glamorous rise and a softer close.

Signature mood: couture city moments and Riviera coastline glamour

Suite mood: elegant, bright, and worthy of a longer pause

Two nights in Paris create the perfect overture, then Cannes and Monaco bring energy, access, and visual drama. Croatia at the end keeps the trip from peaking too early and gives clients a more intimate and restorative final chapter.

Signature moments

  • Paris arrival with immediate hotel glamour and private city pacing
  • Riviera movement by chauffeured car or helicopter where useful
  • Monaco evenings and Cannes daylight glamour without over-scheduling
  • Croatia finish with yacht time, old-town texture, and easier sea rhythms
  • Optional private charter support on select legs

Sample By Day
