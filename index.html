How booking works

The same room, at the same rate, with more waiting when you arrive.

KARCH Luxury Travel Concierge is an affiliate of Departure Lounge, a member of the Virtuoso network. At a large number of hotels that means your stay can be booked with added benefits at no extra cost. You pay what you would have paid booking direct.

On arrival

A better room than the one you paid for

Room upgrade at check-in where availability allows, and your arrival flagged with the hotel rather than treated as one more booking.

Every morning

Breakfast for two, included

Daily breakfast at most participating properties. On a five-night stay that alone is usually worth several hundred dollars.

During the stay

A credit to spend on property

Most hotels add a dining, spa, or resort credit. Yours to use however the stay actually unfolds.

Either end

Early check-in and late checkout

Where the hotel can accommodate it. It is the difference between a travel day that starts well and one that starts in a lobby.

Benefits vary by property and are confirmed before anything is booked. Not every hotel participates, and when one does not, I will tell you rather than talk you into somewhere that does.

Sample Client Proposal

Japan, Raja Ampat, and Hong Kong at a beautifully unhurried pace.

A sample of the kind of polished, privacy-led itinerary KARCH Luxury Travel can design for clients who want exceptional service, rare access, and the freedom to move through each destination without ever feeling rushed.

11 Days / 10 Nights

Aman Tokyo, Southern Japan, a private Raja Ampat yacht, and Rosewood Hong Kong.

This journey is designed around suite accommodations where available, private airport meet-and-greet service, unpacking and packing assistance where offered, layered concierge support, and a calm go-at-your-own-pace rhythm throughout.

Signature Inclusions

  • Private airport meet-and-greet and chauffeured transfers throughout
  • Suite accommodations where available and confirmed
  • Unpacking and packing services where offered by hotel or yacht staff
  • Hotel concierge, onboard yacht concierge, and 24/7 KARCH Luxury Travel support
  • Private charter coordination on request, subject to availability

Days 1-3

Tokyo, Japan

2 nights at Aman Tokyo

Arrive in Tokyo with private airport assistance and settle into a suite at Aman Tokyo, subject to availability. The first day is intentionally left light for rest, spa time, and in-suite ease. The following day may include private cultural experiences such as Iaido, a quietly refined sushi experience, or a gentle river cruise at sunset.

Day 4

Southern Japan

1 night in Kyushu

Continue south for a quieter heritage experience centered on preserved samurai streets, tea culture, and a refined ryokan-style stay. This stop is designed to feel atmospheric and restorative rather than overly programmed.

Days 5-8

Raja Ampat, Indonesia

3 nights aboard a private luxury yacht

Board a private yacht for two full days of world-class diving, hidden lagoon exploration, and barefoot luxury in one of the most extraordinary marine destinations on earth. The extra yacht night preserves a seamless pace and allows the experience to feel immersive rather than rushed.

Days 8-11

Hong Kong

3 nights at Rosewood Hong Kong

Conclude with a polished harbour stay featuring suite accommodations where available, elevated service, wellness, private cultural experiences, and the option to explore a quieter side of Hong Kong by boat or through an early heritage-focused excursion.

Service Philosophy

Go at your own pace.

About KARCH

Meet Harrison Karch.

20+ countries traveled. New York, Dubai, Miami, Istanbul, and Cannes at the top of the list.

Hi, I’m Harrison Karch, born and raised just outside of New York City. I’ve been lucky enough to see a good part of the world, traveling to more than 20 countries, and the places that keep pulling me back are New York, Dubai, Miami, Istanbul, and Cannes.

I have an absolute passion and love for travel. When people say they hate going to the airport, I’m the opposite. I love the airport. I love the feeling that something new is about to happen, whether it is a weekend in Miami, a hotel in Dubai, a summer on the Riviera, or a completely custom trip built around family, privacy, food, golf, yachts, and time well spent.

Why am I doing this?

Because I genuinely love planning trips, and I love showing people a good time whether I am there or not. If I can help create an unparalleled experience with fewer travel headaches, the right accommodations, and access to some of the finest properties in the world, then I’ve done the job right.
