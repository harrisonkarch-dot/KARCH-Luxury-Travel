Mediterranean Grand Tour

Lake Como, Portofino, and the Côte d'Azur with impeccable pacing.

This framework is designed for travelers who want the Mediterranean icons done properly: legendary hotels, private boat access, and the option for helicopter transitions when they make the journey feel lighter and more elegant.

Request This Journey Start Planning
  • Sample pacing: 10 to 14 nights
  • Ideal for couples, celebrations, and milestone summer travel
  • Private aviation and yacht layers available on request

Suggested stays

Passalacqua, Splendido, Hotel du Cap-Eden-Roc, and Cheval Blanc St-Tropez.

Feel

Old-world glamour, polished arrivals, and slow afternoons that turn into luminous evenings.

Best for

Travelers who want icons, but done with quiet access and smooth transitions.

Framework Overview

A summer journey shaped around style, water, and beautiful logistics.

Hotel inspiration: Passalacqua and Riviera grand dame energy

Suite mood: frescoed calm, lake light, and couture detailing

Begin with Lake Como for a graceful reset, continue to Portofino for intimate coastal glamour, and finish in the South of France with private beach clubs, yacht days, and dinners that feel celebratory without ever becoming performative.

Signature moments

  • Private Riva transfers and lake cruising at golden hour
  • Helicopter hops that trade traffic for breathtaking arrival moments
  • After-hours shopping or atelier access where available
  • Yacht day charters along the Ligurian and Riviera coastline
  • Terrace aperitivi and long lunches timed around light, not logistics

Included Style

Service designed to feel invisible in the best way.

Private airport meet-and-greet Chauffeured transfers and heli coordination Yacht and beach club concierge support Suite accommodations where available Packing and unpacking assistance where offered 24/7 KARCH concierge oversight

Sample By Day

An example of how this journey may unfold.

Days 1-3

Lake Como

Arrive privately, settle into a grand lakefront hotel, enjoy slow recovery time, boat cruising, villa gardens, and elegant dinners with the lake as the backdrop.

Days 4-5

Portofino

Continue to Portofino with a beautifully managed transfer, then enjoy private coastal boating, easy lunches, and a softer Ligurian rhythm.

Days 6-8

French Riviera

Transition into the Côte d'Azur with beach clubs, shopping, yacht time, and polished evenings around Cap d'Antibes or St-Tropez.

Days 9-10+

Finish With Ease

Keep the final days open for one more sea day, an additional suite night, or helicopter-assisted departure depending on pace and routing.
