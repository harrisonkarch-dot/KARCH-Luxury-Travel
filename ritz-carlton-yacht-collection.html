Proposal Overview

A yacht-led journey with real destination softness around it.

Signature mood: yacht glamour, sea days, and elevated shore arrivals

Suite mood: light-filled interiors and a calm, polished onboard rhythm

The best version of this trip begins with a hotel chapter that allows guests to arrive properly before embarking, then continues with carefully chosen ports that never feel over-programmed, and closes with one final land stay so the trip ends gracefully rather than abruptly.

Signature moments

  • Pre-cruise city or coast hotel stay with private transfer handling
  • Suite-led yacht experience with curated sea days and shore pacing
  • Private guides, beach clubs, and dining layered only where they add real value
  • Post-cruise decompression at a standout Mediterranean hotel
  • Go-at-your-own-pace structure throughout

Sample By Day

An example of how the yacht proposal may unfold.

Days 1-2

Pre-Cruise Arrival

Arrive into a polished Mediterranean city or coastal hotel, settle in, recover beautifully, and begin with a private dinner or sea-facing suite night.

Days 3-7

Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection

Board the yacht for a run of elegant port days, sea time, curated dining, and carefully selected private shore moments rather than rushed touring.

Day 8

Disembark with Ease

Transition off the yacht into a final hotel stay, ideally somewhere with atmosphere and room to soften the pace.

Days 9-10+

Final Mediterranean Chapter

Finish with one or two indulgent land days built around dining, a beach club, private shopping, or pure suite time before departure.
