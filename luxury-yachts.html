Four Seasons Yachts

Four Seasons service at sea, expressed through intimate residential-style vessels.

Four Seasons Yachts is aimed at affluent travelers who value privacy, generous space, high-touch service, destination access, excellent dining, and a calmer alternative to a large cruise ship.

Four Seasons I: the inaugural expression of Four Seasons hospitality at sea

Suite mood: spacious, residential, and designed around privacy

Four Seasons I

In service since March 2026.

  • 207 meters / 679 feet in length
  • 15 decks and 95 residential-style suites
  • More than 100 ways to connect and configure suites
  • Flexible layouts suited to couples, families, and multigenerational groups
  • High-end dining, wellness, marina access, and personalized destination experiences

Four Seasons II

A more residential second vessel planned for 2028.

Four Seasons II has been announced with 79 suites and an expanded collection of larger residential-style accommodations, while preserving the brand’s intimate scale and personalized service.

  • 79 expansive suites
  • New Yacht Residential Suites with larger living spaces
  • Two- to four-bedroom configurations planned for select accommodations
  • Designed for longer stays, groups, and multigenerational journeys
  • Planned launch in 2028

Hotel-Level Service

Familiar Four Seasons standards.

Personalized attention and a highly staffed service model create the warmth and polish associated with a Four Seasons hotel.

Intimate Scale

A quieter yacht-like atmosphere.

Far fewer suites than a large cruise ship create more space, privacy, and personal recognition throughout the voyage.

Destination Access

Longer stays and smaller harbors.

Itineraries emphasize destination time, flexible exploration, and places that larger ships may not reach as easily.

Residential Comfort

Suites designed for living.

Spacious accommodations, private terraces, thoughtful connections, dining, and wellness suit both couples and families.

Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection

Polished yacht-style cruising with refined hotel chapters before and after sailing.

Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection suits travelers who want a glossy, intimate sea-and-land itinerary with strong suite life, elegant dining, carefully paced port days, and the familiarity of Ritz-Carlton service.

The experience

A natural first step into luxury cruising.

The smaller-scale onboard atmosphere, residential suites, curated dining, and relaxed port rhythm make it especially appealing to couples, celebration travelers, and clients who may be new to cruising.

  • Suite-led onboard experience
  • Sea days balanced with thoughtfully selected ports
  • Private guides, dining, beach clubs, and transfers added where useful
  • Pre- and post-voyage hotel stays that soften each transition
