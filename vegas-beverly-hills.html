Vegas and Beverly Hills

Wynn or Fontainebleau, then The Maybourne Beverly Hills.

This domestic flagship proposal combines the energy and spectacle of Las Vegas with a more polished, shopping-led, suite-focused ending in Beverly Hills for clients who want both buzz and refinement.

Suggested stays

Wynn Las Vegas or Fontainebleau, then The Maybourne Beverly Hills.

Best for

Clients who want a sleek domestic escape without sacrificing suites, service, or visual polish.

Mood

High-energy and glamorous to start, then more tailored and indulgent by the end.

Proposal Overview

Big energy first, then retail, recovery, and Beverly Hills polish.

Signature mood: statement hotels, nightlife access, and polished city transitions

Suite mood: clean, quiet, and perfect for recovery between big moments

Vegas brings the initial burst of energy, restaurants, nightlife, and pool culture. Beverly Hills then reframes the trip around fashion, wellness, and high-touch concierge support with a more tailored pace.

Signature moments

  • Suite arrival at Wynn or Fontainebleau with spa and dining support
  • Poolside recovery, nightlife access, and optional private gaming or show planning
  • Private transfer into Beverly Hills for a mood shift and shopping focus
  • Luxury retail, wellness, and restaurant curation around The Maybourne
  • A domestic itinerary that still feels fully elevated

Sample By Day

An example of how this domestic luxury trip may unfold.

Days 1-3

Las Vegas

Start with a statement suite, strong dining, nightlife access, and enough recovery time that the trip still feels good the next morning.

Day 4

Transition West

Move privately into Beverly Hills with a cleaner, quieter tone and an arrival built around a beautiful suite and easy evening.

Days 5-6

Beverly Hills

Spend the final days around designer shopping, wellness, lunches, and concierge-led access with a more tailored lifestyle feel.

Day 7

Departure
