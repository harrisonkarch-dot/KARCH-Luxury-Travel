How booking works

The same room, at the same rate, with more waiting when you arrive.

KARCH Luxury Travel is part of Departure Lounge and, through that relationship, affiliated with Virtuoso — a global network of leading luxury travel advisors, hotels, cruise lines, tour partners, and destination specialists. At many participating hotels, that connection can add meaningful benefits at no extra cost. You pay what you would have paid booking direct.

On arrival

A better room than the one you paid for

Room upgrade at check-in where availability allows, and your arrival flagged with the hotel rather than treated as one more booking.

Every morning

Breakfast for two, included

Daily breakfast at most participating properties. On a five-night stay that alone is usually worth several hundred dollars.

During the stay

A credit to spend on property

Most hotels add a dining, spa, or resort credit. Yours to use however the stay actually unfolds.

Either end

Early check-in and late checkout

Where the hotel can accommodate it. It is the difference between a travel day that starts well and one that starts in a lobby.

Benefits vary by property and are confirmed before anything is booked. Not every hotel participates, and when one does not, I will tell you rather than talk you into somewhere that does.
