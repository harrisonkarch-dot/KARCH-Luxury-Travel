About KARCH

Meet Harrison Karch.

20+ countries traveled. New York, Dubai, Miami, Istanbul, and Cannes at the top of the list.

Hi, I’m Harrison Karch, born and raised just outside of New York City. I’ve been lucky enough to see a good part of the world, traveling to more than 20 countries, and the places that keep pulling me back are New York, Dubai, Miami, Istanbul, and Cannes.

I have an absolute passion and love for travel. When people say they hate going to the airport, I’m the opposite. I love the airport. I love the feeling that something new is about to happen, whether it is a weekend in Miami, a hotel in Dubai, a summer on the Riviera, or a completely custom trip built around family, privacy, food, golf, culture, and time well spent.

Why am I doing this?

Because I genuinely love planning trips, and I love showing people a good time whether I am there or not. If I can help create an unparalleled experience with fewer travel headaches, the right accommodations, and access to some of the finest properties in the world, then I’ve done the job right.

The goal is for clients to come home saying, “Harrison really helped us with our dream vacation. I can’t wait for the next one.” Travel can always come with unexpected moments, but my job is to anticipate as much as possible, solve what needs solving, and keep the experience feeling easy for you. Ideally, you never even know a problem happened. You just relax, order the next martini, and kick your feet up.
