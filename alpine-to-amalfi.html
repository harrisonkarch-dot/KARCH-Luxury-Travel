Alpine to Amalfi

Swiss mountain glamour giving way to Capri and the Amalfi Coast.

This is a contrast-driven framework for travelers who want crisp mountain air, spa culture, and polished rail or helicopter transitions before easing into a softer coastal chapter in southern Italy.

Request This Journey Start Planning
  • Sample pacing: 9 to 12 nights
  • Ideal for stylish repeat travelers and shoulder-season Europe
  • First-class rail and helicopter layers available on request

Suggested stays

Badrutt's Palace, The Alpina Gstaad, JK Place Capri, and Le Sirenuse.

Feel

Tailored, scenic, and quietly glamorous with strong contrast between the two chapters.

Best for

Couples who love hotels as much as destinations and want each leg to feel distinct.

Framework Overview

Mountain elegance, then southern Italian softness.

Hotel inspiration: grand Alpine houses with spa-driven calm

Suite mood: warm woods, crisp linens, and a couture finish

Start in the Alps with just enough structure to enjoy the scenery without overscheduling, then let the Italian chapter turn more fluid with private boat time, long lunches, and beautifully paced evenings.

Signature moments

  • Panoramic spa time and terrace breakfasts with mountain views
  • Scenic rail or helicopter transitions that make the routing part of the charm
  • Private coastal cruising around Capri and the Amalfi Coast
  • After-hours cultural access or shopping where available
  • Days calibrated to feel polished but never over-managed

Included Style

Designed for travelers who care about both scenery and service.

Visual Highlights

A framework built on contrast, atmosphere, and beautiful transitions.

Sample By Day

An example of how this two-part escape may unfold.

Days 1-3

Swiss Alps

Settle into the mountains with a slower first act built around spa time, scenic breakfasts, and just enough structure to enjoy the landscape beautifully.

Days 4-5

Transition South

Use first-class rail or helicopter support to make the movement itself feel luxurious, not merely practical.

Days 6-8

Capri and Amalfi

Ease into cliffside hotel life with private boat days, terrace lunches, and evenings that feel polished but completely unforced.

Days 9-12

Customize the Finish

Add extra coast time, a cultural stop, or one final suite night depending on whether the trip should end with energy or full relaxation.
